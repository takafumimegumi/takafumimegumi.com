# Writing documents

- Name titles and files after the question, not the answer.
- Give only the deciding reasons, in 2 to 4 sentences.
- Keep each document under about 100 lines.
- Keep unfavorable facts about the chosen option.
- Overwrite documents when a decision changes. Do not keep a change log. Git has the history.
- Do not put progress or next steps in documents.
- Verify costs, limits, and terms against official sources, and record the check date. If you cannot verify a fact, say so.
- When you add or change an ADR, update the list in `docs/adr/README.md`.
